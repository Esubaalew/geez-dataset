# Datasheet

## Contents

- Gold: verb paradigms and noun/adjective rows of *Merho Ges Wesewasew Ze-Yared*. 1,474 records; 10,812 pairs load (train 8,133, val 1,153, test 1,526); 2 rows flagged. Each record keeps its printed page and the raw reading of the entry.
- Unified rows (`data/unified/`): the gold rows and rows of six other books in one schema, each with book and page. 53,969 unified rows; 31,744 load (train 25,678, val 2,650, test 3,416), including gold and book rows; the other 22,225 keep a status and a reason.
- Lexicon, Ge'ez–Amharic pairs, corpora, and synthetic paradigms generated by rule (generator in the `geez-morphology` repository).
- Clitic forms (`data/clitics/`): 98,060 forms with proclitics and enclitics made by rule from the settled corrected rows; each keeps its source row's root, labels and split.
- Corrected rows (`data/unified/geez_morphology_rows_reviewed.jsonl`): the unified rows with corrections to 1,435 held forms applied; 31,744 rows load.

## Method

Gold rows were machine-read from page scans and corrected against the page images, then compared row by row with Yared Shiferaw's typed edition of the same book; rows that do not match are flagged. Rows of the other books come from scans and typed editions. A root is the book's own or the root of the gold record with the same headword; it is not taken from a model or another analyser. Where a gold stem rests on the shape of the form and not on a printed gloss, `docs/CHANGES.md` marks it.

## Splits

A root is in only one of train, val and test. The gold files fix the split of their roots; book rows follow the gold, then a fixed hash rule (`data/unified/README.md`).

## Intended use

Training and scoring root and feature analysis of Classical Ge'ez words. The gold test split measures unseen roots.

## Not for

- Modern Ge'ez, Amharic or Tigrinya morphology.
- Training or scoring on `CHECK`, `NO_ROOT`, `FLAGGED` or `EXCLUDED` rows.
- Treating synthetic forms as attested words.

## Limits

- Only the gold is checked page by page.
- Kidane Wold derivatives whose root no other book gives have one source; they are in train only.
- 8,032 of the 9,220 gold noun and adjective rows have no Amharic gloss.
- Spelling twins count as different roots for the split; 19 such pairs sit in different splits.
- The scans are not distributed.
- Clitic forms are made by rule and are not attested as such; the join is plain concatenation (no sound changes).
- In the corrected rows, a corrected root loads only when settled rows already have it; names and primary nouns without a root do not load, since the schema loads nouns only with a root.
